# Sonora

Control your Sonos S1 and S2 speakers from Home Assistant's sidebar, or from any browser, from any device in your home.

Sonora is a web controller for Sonos: rooms and grouping, music services, your music library, Sonos Favorites and Playlists, EQ, alarms, and sleep timers.

Sonora has a full theming engine with several themes included, among them are close recreations of Sonos' own Windows, Mac and web apps.

Sonora is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sonos, Inc.

Learn more about Sonora [on GitHub](https://github.com/Bretteroo/Sonora)
