# Sonora

Control your Sonos S1 and S2 speakers from Home Assistant's sidebar, or from
any browser in the house.

Sonora is a web controller for Sonos: rooms and grouping, music services,
your music library, Sonos Favorites and Playlists, EQ, alarms and sleep
timers, in twelve themes, among them close recreations of Sonos' own Windows,
Mac and web apps.

Sonora is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sonos, Inc.
